My main strategy follows from my gut instinct that the US market is overvalued, furthermore I did some light research to obtain confirmation bias 😅. This [[Anarkulova et al. - 2023 - Beyond the Status Quo A Critical Assessment of Lifecycle Investment Advice.pdf|paper]] and its companion [video analysis](https://youtu.be/-nPon8Ad_Ug?si=6IJCld9XL37v8BoZ&t=434) discusses how to *optimally* to split your investments across different markets. The key takeaways are : 
- Following the [[Anarkulova et al. - 2023 - Beyond the Status Quo A Critical Assessment of Lifecycle Investment Advice.pdf#page=89|last plot]] of the paper 😂, the optimal strategy is 33% in domestic and 67% in foreign equities is optimal if you do not condition on being a US investor.
- Even if you condition on being a US investor, but you are only 50% confident that US exceptionalism is going to continue, then it is optimal to have $ \approx 60\%/40\%$ allocation domestic vs foreign respectively, see [[Anarkulova et al. - 2023 - Beyond the Status Quo A Critical Assessment of Lifecycle Investment Advice.pdf#page=87|Table C.VII]]. Foreign markets have an index called MSCI (analogous to the SNP).
